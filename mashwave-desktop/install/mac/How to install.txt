ULTIMATE MEDIA MANAGER - for Mac computers
==========================================

WHAT YOU NEED
- A Mac with an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3 or newer - Macs sold since late
  2020). It does not run on older Macs with an Intel chip.
- The free VLC player, installed in your Applications folder. Get it
  from videolan.org. Ultimate Media Manager uses it to play TV, music
  and video.
- For TV: the sign-in details from your TV provider. The app has no
  channels of its own.

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Install VLC first if you do not have it (videolan.org).

2. Double-click "UltimateMediaManager-Mac.dmg". A window opens.

3. Drag "Ultimate Media Manager" onto the Applications folder in that
   window.

4. Open it from Applications. The first time, the Mac may say it
   cannot check the app, or that it is from an unidentified developer.
   This is shown for any app that is not from the App Store.
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the
     line about Ultimate Media Manager, and click "Open Anyway".
   - On older versions of macOS you can instead hold Control, click
     the app, choose "Open", then "Open" again.
   You only do this once.

FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
1. Click "Add your TV service".
2. Type the server address, user name and password from your TV
   provider - or a playlist link.
   Quicker: copy the whole message your provider sent you, then click
   "Paste" and the app fills the boxes in for you.
3. Your channels, movies and shows appear after a moment.

WHAT ELSE IT DOES
Down the left side, under "On this computer":
- Music: your songs, automatic mashups, and two decks for mixing.
- My videos: the video files on this computer.
- Photo editor: crop, adjust, looks, touch-up, blur, draw.
- Video editor: join and cut clips, add words and music, save a video.
- Play on a TV: send what is playing to a smart TV on your Wi-Fi.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Your sign-in, favourites and lists stay on your own computer.
- To update, drag the new version into Applications and choose
  "Replace". Your sign-in and favourites are kept.
- To remove it, drag it from Applications to the Bin.
