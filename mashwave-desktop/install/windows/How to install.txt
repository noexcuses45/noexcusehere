ULTIMATE MEDIA MANAGER - for Windows computers
==============================================

WHAT YOU NEED
- A computer with Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
- For TV: the sign-in details from your TV provider. The app has no
  channels of its own.
- Nothing else. The video player is built in.

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Double-click "UltimateMediaManager-Setup.msi".

2. Windows may show a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC".
   It shows this for programs it has not seen many times before.
   Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".

3. Follow the steps on screen. When it has finished you will find
   "Ultimate Media Manager" on your desktop and in the Start menu.

FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
1. Click "Add your TV service".
2. Type the server address, user name and password from your TV
   provider - or a playlist link.
   Quicker: copy the whole message your provider sent you, then click
   "Paste" and the app fills the boxes in for you.
3. Your channels, movies and shows appear after a moment.

WHAT ELSE IT DOES
Down the left side, under "On this computer":
- Music: your songs, automatic mashups, and two decks for mixing.
- My videos: the video files on this computer.
- Photo editor: crop, adjust, looks, touch-up, blur, draw.
- Video editor: join and cut clips, add words and music, save a video.
- Play on a TV: send what is playing to a smart TV on your Wi-Fi.
The first time you play something on a TV, Windows asks whether to
let the app through its firewall. Choose "Allow".

GOOD TO KNOW
- Your sign-in, favourites and lists stay on your own computer.
- To update, run the new installer. It replaces the old version and
  keeps your sign-in and favourites.
- To remove it: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps >
  Ultimate Media Manager > Uninstall.
